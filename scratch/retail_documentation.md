## Game Overview

**Dragon's Dogma Online (DDON)** was a massively multiplayer online role-playing game (MMORPG) developed and published by Capcom for PlayStation 3, PlayStation 4, and Microsoft Windows.

### Key Dates
- **Launch:** August 31, 2015 (after several alpha and beta tests)
- **Service Discontinuation Announced:** July 2019
- **Servers Shut Down:** December 5, 2019

### Core Features
- Free-to-play (F2P) with item-based microtransactions
- Cross-platform play between PS3, PS4, and PC
- Real-time action-based combat system similar to Dragon's Dogma
- Pawn companion system
- The scenario was written as a set of seasons similar to a television series, originally planned for five years (ended at Season 3 due to discontinuation)

## Vocation/Job System

### Starting Vocations
Players can select one of four starting vocations when beginning the game:
- **Fighter** (Attacker)
- **Hunter** (Attacker)
- **Priest** (Support)
- **Shield Sage** (Tank)

### Vocation Types
- **Attacker:** Offensive DPS roles
- **Support:** Healing and buff roles
- **Tank:** Defensive roles

### Key Differences from Dragon's Dogma: Dark Arisen
- Vocations are leveled up independently
- Stat gains from one vocation have no effect on other vocations
- Augments from one vocation can still be equipped on another

### Additional Vocations (Unlocked via Story)
Other vocations become available upon completing the first Main Quest. As of Season 3.2, all vocations except High Scepter become available after completing "Resolutions and Omens."

Additional vocations introduced over seasons include:
- Sorcerer (unlocked via Job Master)
- Seeker (unlocked via Job Master)
- Spirit Lancer (introduced in Season 2.2)

### Switching Vocations
Players can switch vocations by speaking to **Archibald** at the White Dragon Temple.

### Skills System

#### Custom Skills and Abilities
- Skills can be unlocked up to Level 5 at innkeepers and Archibald
- Level 6+ skills require training with respective **Job Masters**

#### Abilities (Augments)
- Similar to Augments in DD/DA
- Locked to Vocation levels
- Equipped using Ability Cost points (maximum 15 in alpha)
- Each Ability requires different Ability Cost points

### Job Masters
- One Job Master exists for each vocation
- Access requires reaching Level 18 in a vocation
- Speak to **Renton** at White Dragon Temple to receive a Personal Quest
- Job Masters assign simple quests (killing specific enemies) before unlocking Level 6+ skills

## Pawn System

### Main Pawns
- First Main Pawn created upon completing Main Quest "A Servant's Pledge"
- Up to **three Main Pawns** can be created
- Second Main Pawn requires 10 Riftstone Shards presented to the White Dragon
- Third Main Pawn requires completing third tier of Dragon Force Augmentation skill tree
- Main Pawns can be deleted and replaced (requires another 10 Riftstone Shards)

### Support Pawns
- Hired via Riftstone
- Can assist in battle or craft equipment
- Limited usage before needing to be released and re-hired
- Hiring costs Rift Crystals (R)
- Rating and returning Support Pawns earns Rental Points (RP)
- RP can be converted into Job Points (JP) for Main Pawns

### Partner Pawn
- Special designation for one Main Pawn
- Appears in the Arisen's Room
- Used for room management functions

### Pawn Mechanics

#### Pawn Orders
The Arisen can give commands directly to party pawns via the Pawn Orders menu.

#### Item Usage
Pawns cannot use items independently. When the Arisen uses an item, effects apply to pawns as well (e.g., curing Poisoned status).

#### Pawn Recovery
- Fallen Main Pawns become "Lost" and require Pawn Rescue mission to resurrect
- Adventure Passport holders can resurrect Main Pawns at any Riftstone by paying R
- Support Pawns can be re-summoned via Riftstone if usage hasn't expired

#### Pawn Skills
- Main Pawns earn Job Points (JP) when leveling
- Skills upgraded at Inns or Job Masters
- Main Pawns have their own Dragon Force Augmentation skill tree (different from Arisen's)

## Quest System

### Quest Types

#### Main Quests
- Required to advance the storyline
- Unlock certain functions and areas
- Additional vocations unlocked after first Main Quest completion

#### World Quests
- Multiplayer-exclusive quests
- Up to 8 players cooperate during the quest
- Dungeon World Quests have "Door of" in their names (e.g., "Door of Dragon Force: The Beast's Lair")

#### Solo Quests
- Single-player content
- One player can complete alone

#### Personal Quests
- Individual character progression quests
- Includes Area Trials for Area Rank advancement

#### Board Quests
- Posted on notice boards in each area
- Marked as Personal Quests (Green) in Quest Log
- Give Experience (XP), Gold (G), Rift Crystals (R), and Area Points (AP)
- Most are repeatable
- Difficulty and rewards vary by notice board location

### Quest Rewards
Upon quest completion, players receive:
- Experience Points (XP)
- Gold (G)
- Rift Crystals (R)

### Area Points and Area Ranks
- Completing Board Quests and World Quests earns Area Points (AP)
- Speak to local Area Master to increase Area Rank when enough AP accumulated
- Higher Area Ranks unlock:
  - New locations
  - New quests
  - New special enemies
- Area Ranks 5, 10, 13, or 15 require completing special Area Trials

### Notice Board Difficulty (Highest to Lowest Rewards)
1. Lynwood
2. Mysree Forest
3. Pawn Cathedral
4. Pawn Settlement
5. Rotes
6. Breya Coast
7. Tel
8. Hidell Plains
9. White Dragon Temple

## Combat Mechanics

### Core Combat System
- Real-time action-based battle system
- Similar to Dragon's Dogma: Dark Arisen
- Enemies have particular vulnerabilities to different classes
- Giant enemies can be climbed by the Arisen

### Weak Points
- Each enemy has weak points for high damage
- Some boss encounters rely on weak point mechanics

### Enemy Meters
- **Fatigue Meter:** Relates to enemy energy
- **Stamina Meter:** Relates to attack power
- Depleting these meters allows players to deal higher damage

### Elemental System
Based on the Dragon's Dogma system, enemies have resistances and weaknesses to:
- Fire
- Ice
- Thunder
- Holy
- Dark

Physical damage types:
- Slashing Damage
- Blunt Damage

## Shop and Economy Systems

### Currency Types

| Currency | Japanese | Description |
|----------|----------|-------------|
| **Gold (G)** | ゴールド | Primary currency for shops and services |
| **Rift Crystals (R)** | リム | Used for hiring Support Pawns, premium services |
| **Blood Orbs (BO)** | ブラッドオーブ | Used for Dragon Force Augmentation and rare items |
| **Area Points (AP)** | エリアポイント | Regional progression currency |
| **Rental Points (RP)** | レンタルポイント | Earned from pawn rentals, converts to JP |
| **Job Points (JP)** | ジョブポイント | Used for skill purchases |

### Bazaar (Player Trading)

#### Overview
- Player marketplace for materials
- Located in White Dragon Temple (multiple representatives)
- Higher prices than NPC shops

#### Selling
- Only Materials category items can be sold
- Maximum 5 items at a time (10 with Adventure Passport)
- 24-hour listing duration
- Minimum and maximum price limits
- Commission taken on sales

#### Buying
- Category Search by category and item rank
- Free Word Search by item name
- Items can be purchased before you can grab them

### Cash Shop and Premium Services
- Introduced with Season 2.2 updates
- Adventure Passport provides various benefits:
  - Increased Bazaar listing slots (10 instead of 5)
  - Faster Main Pawn resurrection (pay R at Riftstone instead of Pawn Rescue)
  - Increased XP/BO multipliers

### Riftstone Shard Shop
- Item shop sells Riftstone Shards
- 10 shards for 4,000G (400G each)
- Required for obtaining additional Main Pawns

## Crafting System

### Overview
- Requires raw materials and Gold (G)
- Missing materials may be available at Bazaar
- Equipment can be enhanced with stars (each level indicated by a star)

### Pawn Crafting

#### Requirements
Complete Personal Quest "Crafted Token of The Heart" (クラフトで伝える心)

#### Crafting Levels
- Main Pawns earn crafting experience when crafting
- Leveling up crafting level unlocks Craft Skill points
- Five crafting specialties available

#### Crafting Process
- One Main Pawn designated as crafting team "leader"
- Pawns participating in crafting are dismissed from party
- Cannot be summoned until crafting completes
- Partner Pawn still appears in Arisen's Room during crafting

### Equipment Enhancement
- Multiple attempts may be needed for full enhancement
- Each enhancement level indicated by a star

### Crests
- Mounted on equipment to strengthen or add effects
- Equipment colors can be changed with special dyes

### Craftable Items
- Weapons
- Armor
- Consumables
- Furniture (for Arisen's Room)

## Dragon Force Augmentation

### Overview
Skill tree system that provides permanent passive bonuses.

### Unlock Requirement
Complete Main Quest "The Crimson Crystal" (紅の結晶)

### Blood Orb Currency
- Obtained from defeating **Orb Enemies** (enemies with purple health bar)
- Higher level Orb Enemies give more Blood Orbs
- Maximum 50,000 Blood Orbs can be held
- Also used to purchase rare items from **Gilstan, The Goblin King**

### Skill Tree Categories
1. **Vitality**
2. **Combat**
3. **Adventure**
4. **Magick**

### Progression Rules
- Once a perk is selected in a category, only adjacent perks can be chosen
- Central "trunk" options require high total Vocation levels
- Perks apply to all vocations

### Separate Trees
- Arisen has their own tree
- Each Main Pawn has their own individual tree
- Pawn trees have different augments/passives than Arisen's tree

## Grand Missions and Extreme Missions

### Grand Missions (GM)

#### Overview
- Instanced, timed missions
- Up to 8 players queue and cooperate
- Goal: Defeat as many enemies as possible within time limit
- More points = better rewards

#### Scheduling
- Usually available during weekends
- Scheduled events
- Certain Grand Missions only available from specific NPCs

#### Examples
- The Ancient Warrior (Level 45)
- The Crucible of Demons (Level 45)
- Gritten Fort Battle (Level 45)
- Bloodbane Isle's Feast of Madness

### Extreme Missions (EXM)

#### Overview
- High-difficulty quests
- Maximum 4 party members
- Must defeat enemies within stipulated time limit

#### Unique Rules
- **No personal consumables allowed**
- Mission-only items provided for duration
- Must complete EXMs in order (can't skip ahead)

#### Requirements
1. Complete Main Quest: "Be Forevermore, White Dragon" (白竜よ永遠なれ)
2. Complete Personal Quest: "The Dragon Force's Whirl" (竜力の渦)

#### Participation
- Talk to **Seneka** to participate
- Similar procedure to Grand Mission participation

## Arisen Room Features

### Location
Personal room accessible from the White Dragon Temple.

### Basic Features
- **Bath:** Provides healing and 30-minute buff (~30% boost to health and stamina)
- **Storage Chest:** 3 additional pages with 10 tabs each
- **Riftstone:** For pawn summoning
- **Mini Portcrystal:** For fast travel
- **Training Area:** Accessible from room

### Furniture System

#### Obtaining Recipes
- Earned as Achievement rewards
- Redeem via Achievements menu > Furniture Recipes
- Some items obtained via NPC exchange

#### Crafting Furniture
- Crafted in Craft Room
- Each piece can only be crafted once

#### Placing Furniture
1. Speak to Partner Pawn
2. Select "Room Management" (自室の管理)
3. Select area and item to place

#### Furniture Types
- Closets
- Tables
- Table lamps
- Foodstuff decorations
- And more

### Mandragora Cultivation
- Introduced in Season 3.0
- "Rearing" of Mandragora in Arisen's Room
- Additional feature for resource gathering

### Room Clothing
- Loungewear for Arisen and Partner Pawn
- Introduced in Season 2.2

## Multiplayer and Party System

### Hub Area
- **White Dragon Temple:** Main hub where all players on current world visible
- Worlds numbered 1-50
- English players typically on World 13

### Party Size
- Maximum 4 players/pawns in world zones (dungeons, caves, etc.)
- Grand Missions: Up to 8 players

### Cross-Platform Play
Full cross-play between:
- PlayStation 3
- PlayStation 4
- PC (Windows)

### Instance Types

#### World Zones
- Dungeons, caves, field areas
- Limited to party members only

#### Hub Zones
- White Dragon Temple
- All players on same world visible
