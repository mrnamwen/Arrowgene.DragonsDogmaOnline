# DDON Retail Cash Shop Research

This document contains research findings about Dragon's Dogma Online's cash shop systems as they operated in the retail version (before service ended December 5, 2019).

## Premium Currency System

### Golden Gemstones (黄金石)
The primary premium currency in DDON.

**Pricing:**
- Purchased via CAP (Capcom's electronic currency)
- 1 CAP = 1 JPY
- 1 Golden Gemstone = 100 CAP (100 JPY / ~$1 USD)
- Bulk purchases offered discounts
- Minimum purchase: 300 CAP

**Payment Methods:**
- Credit cards
- Japanese e-money services (BitCash, WebMoney)
- Prepaid cards
- Mobile payments

### Silver Tickets
A secondary currency that could be obtained through:
- Login bonuses
- Daily missions
- Special events
- Some Silver Ticket-only gacha were available

## GP Courses (Premium Subscription Services)

GP Courses provided time-limited gameplay bonuses. Multiple courses could be activated simultaneously for stacking bonuses.

### 1. Adventure Passport (冒険パスポート)
**Cost:** 15 Golden Gemstones / 30 days (~1,500 JPY/month)

**Benefits:**
- Infinite revival gemstone restoration (unlimited revives)
- Expanded storage box capacity (+240 item slots)
- Free fast travel to personal base (Arisen's Room)
- Doubled support items from area masters (200%)
- Quest slots increased from 10 to 20
- Bazaar exhibit slots increased (+5)
- Bazaar re-exhibit time shortened by 1 day
- 50% warp point teleportation discount
- Free inn stays (100% discount)
- 1.5x Pawn crafting experience (150%)
- Faster pawn revival animation (50% shorter)
- RP-based pawn revival enabled
- Access to all warp points
- Extra storage tabs enabled
- Craft items consumable from storage box

### 2. Growth Support Course (成長サポートコース)
**Cost:** 4 Golden Gemstones / 3 hours OR 8 Golden Gemstones / 5x 1-hour tickets

**Benefits:**
- 2x Experience from enemies (200%)
- 2x Experience from Board Quests (200%)
- 2x Experience from World Quests (200%)
- 1.5x Area Points from quests (150%)
- 2x Pawn Experience (200%)
- 2x PP (Play Points) acquisition (200%)
- Disables party EXP adjustment penalty

### 3. Reward Support Course (報酬サポートコース)
**Cost:** 4 Golden Gemstones / 3 hours OR 8 Golden Gemstones / 5x 1-hour tickets

**Benefits:**
- 2x Quest reward quality/rarity (200%)
- Special quest rewards enabled
- 2x Rim rewards from Board Quests (200%)
- 2x Rim rewards from World Quests (200%)
- 2x Enemy drop rates (200%)
- 2x Gathering item quantities (200%)
- 1.5x Blood Orb acquisition (150%)
- 2x Extreme Mission rewards (200%)

### 4. Reliable Assist Course (安心アシストコース)
**Cost:** Similar pricing to Support Courses (available in 7-day packs for 20 GG)

**Benefits:**
- 50% incoming damage reduction
- 125% Endurance/Stamina
- 50% debuff damage reduction
- Lever gacha assist enabled (66% success boost)
- 350% faster automatic pawn rescue

### 5. Grand Mission Course (グランドミッションコース)
**Cost:** 6 Golden Gemstones / 72 hours

**Benefits:**
- 2x Score earnings in Grand Missions
- 2x Job Points in Grand Missions
- Easier to reach high-rank cumulative rewards

### 6. Storage Chest Passports (格納チェストパスポート)
**Cost:** Varied by tier

**Benefits:**
- Additional 400 item storage slots per passport tier
- Multiple tiers available (Passport 1, 2, 3)

### 7. Special Event Courses
- **5x Subjugation EXP Course:** 5x enemy experience
- **3x/5x PP Acquisition Course:** Multiplied Play Point gains
- **Internet Cafe Special Course:** Combined benefits of multiple courses (for net cafe players)

### Course Stacking Bonuses
When multiple courses were active simultaneously:
- **Double Activation Benefit:** 2.5x EXP multipliers
- **Triple Activation Benefit:** 3x EXP multipliers, 3x drops, 3x blood orbs

## Gacha System (Treasure Lots / トレジャーズロット)

### Standard Treasure Lots
**Cost:**
- Single pull: 3 Golden Gemstones (300 JPY)
- 11-pull: 30 Golden Gemstones (3,000 JPY) - one free pull

**Alternative Currency:**
- Some lots accepted Silver Tickets (30 tickets per pull)

### Rarity Rates (Standard Lots)
| Grade | Items | Rate |
|-------|-------|------|
| S-Rank | Weapons, rare equipment | 4.98% |
| A-Rank | Crests (weapon enchantments) | 34.93% |
| B-Rank | Consumables, enhancement materials | 60.01% |

### Types of Treasure Lots

1. **Standard Weapon/Equipment Lots**
   - Featured job-specific weapons
   - Seasonal equipment sets
   - Limited-time cosmetics

2. **Crest Rush (クレストラッシュ)**
   - Cost: 1 GG single / 10 GG for 11-pull
   - 11-pull guaranteed at least one S-rank crest
   - Crests added elemental/status effects to weapons

3. **Guaranteed Equipment Lots**
   - "Knight Company Fun Bags" style lots
   - Guaranteed complete armor sets
   - Gender-specific versions (male/female)
   - All items at maximum enhancement (4-star)

4. **High IR Equipment Lots**
   - Featured IR112/IR115 weapons
   - 13-play packages with guaranteed rewards
   - Campaign coins for exchange shop

## Box Gacha System (ボックストレジャーズロット)

A special gacha format where each reward can only be obtained once until reset.

### Mechanics
- Contains a fixed pool of rewards (e.g., 42 items)
- Each reward can only be won once (no duplicates)
- Drawing continues until all rewards obtained or manual reset

### Reset Feature
- **Reset Button:** Restores all items to initial state
- **Unlimited resets:** No limit on reset count
- **Full reset only:** Cannot reset individual items
- **Irreversible:** Cannot undo a reset

### Example: Autumn Variety Lot (秋のバラエティロット)
- 42 total rewards in the box
- Silver Ticket only (no Golden Gemstones)
- Daily login + missions provided 30 Silver Tickets
- 14 days x 30 tickets = 420 tickets total
- Without resetting, could obtain all 42 items

### Strategy
- Players could reset early to target specific rare items
- Or complete the entire box for guaranteed full collection
- Box gacha was generally more player-friendly than standard gacha

## Online Shop (オンラインショップ)

### Direct Purchase Items

1. **Beauty Salon Services**
   - Cost: 5 Golden Gemstones per use
   - Change Arisen/Pawn appearance
   - Reusable service

2. **Pawn Voices**
   - Cost: 9 Golden Gemstones each
   - Featured popular voice actors
   - Permanent purchase, usable on multiple pawns
   - Gender-locked (male voices for male pawns only)

3. **Craft Master Pawn Services**
   - Cost: 5 Golden Gemstones / 30 days
   - Three specialization types:
     - Consumables specialist
     - High-quality equipment specialist
     - Enhancement specialist
   - No RC cost for crafting
   - Higher quality crafted items

4. **Character Customization Tickets**
   - Custom Made Service tickets
   - Obtained through campaigns/purchases

### Free Daily Items
- Special events offered 0 GG items daily
- One per character, time-limited
- Could not be retroactively claimed

### Item Post System
- Purchased/reward items delivered to Item Post
- Retrieved via: Game Menu > Online Shop > Item Post

| Silver Tickets | Login/events/missions | Some gacha, limited purchases |
| CAP | Real money | Purchase Golden Gemstones |
| Rift Crystals (RC) | Gameplay, pawn rentals | Craft Master Pawn bypass |

## Free-to-Play Viability

According to Capcom's official statements:
- Core gameplay fully accessible without payment
- "Exploring the world, battling monsters, and acquiring/crafting equipment" available free
- High-tier gear obtainable through regular play
- Premium services provided convenience, not power advantages
- Gacha items were largely cosmetic or provided moderate stat boosts

## Implementation Notes for Server Emulator

The existing codebase already has:
1. **GP Course System:** Fully defined in `GpCourseInfo.json` with 18 course types and 60+ effects
2. **Effect IDs:** Mapped to specific gameplay modifiers (EXP boosts, drop rates, etc.)
3. **Custom Courses:** Support for server-defined courses (e.g., "Arrowgene Blessing")

Still needed for full retail parity:
1. **Gacha System:** Treasure Lot mechanics, rate tables, box gacha reset logic
2. **Premium Currency:** Golden Gemstone tracking and transactions
3. **Online Shop UI:** Direct item purchases, Item Post delivery
4. **Silver Ticket System:** Alternative currency tracking
