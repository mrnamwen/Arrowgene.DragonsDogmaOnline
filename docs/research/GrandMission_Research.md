# Dragon's Dogma Online - Grand Mission (GM) Research

## Overview

Grand Missions (グランドミッション / GM) are large-scale 8-player raid-style content in Dragon's Dogma Online. Players queue for instanced, timed missions where they defeat enemies to accumulate points. Higher point totals yield better rewards.

**Key Characteristics:**
- 8-player party content (pawns NOT allowed - real players only)
- Instanced and timed missions
- Score-based reward system
- Available during specific time windows (typically weekends)
- Different NPCs host different Grand Missions

---

## Mechanics

### Participation Requirements
1. Complete prerequisite quests (varies by mission)
2. Register through **Lestania News** (press square/4 button) or designated NPCs
3. Form 8-player parties via Entry Boards with customizable filters

### Registration Process
1. Talk to designated NPC (e.g., Zelkin at harbor cave, Alan at Central Plaza)
2. Select "Mission Entry" (ミッションエントリー)
3. Choose your "Battle Status" (戦況)
4. Entry Board opens for party registration/search
5. When 8 members gather, select "Ready" (準備完了) within time limit
6. All 8 participants teleport to harbor cave in White Dragon Temple
7. Talk to Zelkin again, select "Mission Entry"
8. When all 8 select "Sortie" (出撃), the Grand Mission begins

### Time Windows
- **Preparation Period**: Before the main operation
- **Main Operation Period**: Active gameplay window
- **Analysis Period**: Post-mission processing
- **Reward Collection Period**: Claim rewards from NPC

Example schedule (Dec 2015):
- Preparation: Dec 31, 2015 11:00-16:00 JST
- Main Operation: Dec 31, 2015 16:00 to Jan 4, 2016 16:00 JST
- Analysis: Jan 4, 16:00-17:00 JST
- Reward Collection: Jan 4, 17:00 until Jan 7 maintenance

### Combat Mechanics

**Rage/Stamina System:**
- Bosses enter rage state after taking enough damage (stamina bar appears below HP)
- Support vocations (Element Archer, Priest, Spirit Lancer) can expose weak points
- Hitting weak points with corresponding elements is highly effective

**Break Mechanic (Season 2+):**
- Continuously hitting boss during rage builds blue icon on left of HP bar
- When fully blue, massive stamina damage dealt
- Stamina bar turns blue - pulling becomes more effective
- Boss enters tired state when stamina depleted

## Rewards System

### Immediate Rewards
- Potions
- Golden Orbs
- Job Points (amount varies by mission level)

**Note:** Players do NOT receive gold or experience points from Grand Missions.

### Score-Based Rewards

Example from 魔物のるつぼ (Monster's Crucible):
- **4000+ points**: ベヘモットフング, アルケムウッド (equipment materials)
- **12000+ points**: なめらかな竜革, 分厚い竜鱗, 霊鉄石の破片 (Lv51+ equipment production materials)

### Cumulative Score Rewards
Based on total score collected during event period:
- 白翼輝晶 (White Wing Crystal) - Season 1.2 top-tier armor material
- 大きな竜骨石 (Large Dragon Bone Stone)
- 欠けた竜骨石 (Chipped Dragon Bone Stone)
- 鱗砂 (Scale Sand)
- 大粒の鱗砂 (Large Scale Sand)
- 古層の鱗砂 (Ancient Layer Scale Sand)

### General Reward Philosophy
- GMs reward materials for top-tier **armors** of the season
- EMs (Extreme Missions) reward materials for top-tier **weapons**
- Some GMs yield significant gold in lower difficulty versions

## Grand Mission vs Extreme Mission Comparison

| Feature | Grand Mission (GM) | Extreme Mission (EXM) |
|---------|-------------------|----------------------|
| Party Size | 8+ players | Maximum 4 players |
| Pawns Allowed | No | No |
| Focus | Large-scale score-based | High-difficulty specific objectives |
| Personal Items | Standard usage | Cannot use; mission-only items provided |
| Rare Item Limit | Cumulative during event | 1 rare item per day maximum |
| Availability | Scheduled time windows (weekends) | After clearing specific quests |
| Main Rewards | Armor crafting materials | Weapons, Custom Skills |

## Mission-Specific Strategies

### Gritten Fortress Siege (グリッテン砦攻防戦)
- Blue shield markers on map indicate enemy spawn locations
- Distinguish between main units and surprise raid units (marked with "+")
- Defeat surprise units first to prevent disappearance and maximize points

### Monster's Crucible (魔物のるつぼ)
Two scoring strategies:
1. **Quick Method**: Earn 1000-1500 points by defeating low-level dragons
2. **Extended Method**: Achieve 4000+ points by defeating all "time keepers" to extend time limits

**Tip:** Grand Mission Course can double points when active

### Ancient Temple Death Battle (古代神殿の死闘)
- Basic parties split into left and right paths
- Entry board comments often indicate intended strategy
- Review party leader instructions before engagement
